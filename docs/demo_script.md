# SkillTrack 10-minute demo script

1. **Problem — 45 sec:** explain opaque screening, weak resume/JD signals and limited placement-office visibility.
2. **Home — 45 sec:** show public KPIs and explain that they are database-derived.
3. **Rohan — 2 min:** quick-login as Rohan, upload the generated sample PDF, inspect canonical skills, open ML Engineer fit and show missing `scikit-learn`, `Docker`, `AWS` plus the CGPA eligibility flag.
4. **Aarav — 1.5 min:** login as Aarav, open Data Science Intern, show matched evidence and the computed fit score, then apply. The application tracker refreshes while scoring is pending.
5. **Priya — 2 min:** open Applicants, inspect a ranked candidate, toggle blind mode and show that identity fields disappear server-side. Shortlist a candidate.
6. **Notifications — 45 sec:** return to the affected student and show the notification plus application history entry.
7. **Admin — 1.5 min:** approve the pending Vivek Digital Labs company, inspect database-derived KPIs and demand/supply, then open ML Insights and point out the synthetic-data label and evaluation artifacts.
8. **Architecture — 1 min:** explain JWT, bcrypt, PDF parsing, taxonomy evidence, TF-IDF/optional embeddings, weighted scoring and the zero-config SQLite runtime.

Use `POST /api/v1/demo/reset` from the admin account to restore the seeded state.
